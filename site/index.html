About Dev Catalog

Curated Developer Tooling

A curated, rated catalog of developer scripts, tools, and workflows, with source, license, platform support, and requirements recorded for each. Read directly by agents via llms.txt or the dev-catalog plugin skill.

Shortcuts: ⌘ / Ctrl K commands · / filter catalog · ↑↓ navigate · Esc reset

Rubric & Evidence Levels

  • Score: seven grades from 0 to 4, weighted by practical value (25%), dependability (20%), ease of use (15%), safety and control (15%), adoption cost (10%), portability (10%) and upkeep (5%). Weighted total / 40 gives a score out of 10. Higher is better on every axis.
  • Compare fairly: score sorting puts complete ratings first, incomplete ratings next, and entries with blockers last. Incomplete ratings have no total; blockers cannot be cleared by a high average. Check platform fit and evidence separately.
  • Evidence tiers: reviewed (curator read) → used (used in practice) → tested (linked test).
  • Source: original/adapted (authored here), vendored (pinned upstream copy), or linked (external project).
